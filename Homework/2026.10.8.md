Focused mode and Diffuse mode

### Explain
- Focused mode: the brain concentrates intensely on a difficult and specific question
- Diffuse mode: the brain is in a relaxed state in which the mind wanders freely

### Apply
- One activity can involve two modes at different times (drawing)
- Focused mode: painters use focused mode for practices, like analyzing the relationship of light and shadow, or positional relationship
- Diffuse mode: painters use diffuse mode to get inspiration by letting the mind wander

### Transfer
What should you do if you are studying for a difficult exam and become stuck on a problem?
- I will take a short break because continuing using focused mode can only work further on the wrong path. Instead, having a break gives the brain a chance to wander freely and get inspiration or connection for a solving method.
