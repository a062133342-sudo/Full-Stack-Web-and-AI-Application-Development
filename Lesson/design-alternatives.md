### What the recommended order of brainstorming, individual to group or vice versa and why?
- First in individual and then group because for the individual process, it is more likely to generate ideas.

### Write your 5 tips for individual brainstorming with explanations in your own word
1. write down the core problem: Find and write the main thing need to solve
2. constrain yourself: set goal and limits (time)
3. aim for 20: think 20 ideas
4. take a break: relax
5. divide and conquer: Split the big hard problem into small parts and solve each part at a time.

### What are the challenges we might come cross in group brainstorming?
- Conformity: the tendency to agree with or follow the group's reasoning and ideas
- Production Blocking: The tendency of some individuals in discussions to block other individuals' participation
- Power dynamics: The tendency to defer to more senior individuals, or to overpower less senior individuals

### List the 5 tips for group brainstorming and explain in your own word
1. Go through every individual idea: Look at each idea once a time
2. Find the optimal size: Work out the best size for your group
3. Set clear rules for communication: Set simple rules for talking and sharing
4. Set clear expectations: Set goal
5. End with ideas, not decisions: Finish the meeting by collecting ideas

### how do the tips of both individual and group brainstorming help deal with the challenges for brainstorming?
- For individual brainstorming, it lets people think their own ideas first, which can avoid conformity and stops people from following others. For group brainstorming, its rules make sure everyone’s ideas get heard, which reduce production blocking. This combination benefits the brainstorming process and increase productivity.
